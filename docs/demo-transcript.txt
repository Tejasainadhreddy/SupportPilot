## 1. Policy question -> KnowledgeMCP over MCP, with a citation
customer> What is the policy on digital gift cards?
assistant> Digital gift cards are non-refundable [S1]. Sources: [S1] Refund Policy (section 'Refund Policy > Eligibility')
   status=complete  tools=['knowledge.ask_question']
   nodes: guard_input > classify > knowledge > respond

## 2. Missing refund details are collected over turns
customer> I'd like a refund please
assistant> To start a refund I need your order number (like ORD-1234) and the reason for the refund.
   status=complete  tools=[]
   nodes: guard_input > classify > refund_prepare > respond
customer> ORD-1001, the mouse arrived broken
assistant> Your refund RF-DDE17492 of $45.00 for order ORD-1001 has been issued. It will reach your original payment method in 5 to 7 business days.
   status=complete  tools=['get_order', 'issue_refund']
   nodes: guard_input > classify > refund_prepare > execute_refund > respond

## 3. Refund above $100 pauses for a human (LangGraph interrupt)
customer> Refund ORD-1002, the keyboard is defective
assistant> Your refund request of $249.99 for order ORD-1002 needs approval from a support lead, usually within one business day. You'll get an update here.
   status=pending_approval  tools=['get_order']
   nodes: guard_input > classify > refund_prepare > notify_pending
approver> GET /approvals  ->  ORD-1002  $249.99  (item arrived damaged or defective)

   status=complete  tools=['human_approval', 'issue_refund']

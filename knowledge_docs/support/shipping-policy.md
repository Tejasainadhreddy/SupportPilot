# Shipping Policy

## Delivery times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders over 50 US dollars ship free with standard shipping.

## Tracking

A tracking link is emailed when the order ships. Customers can also ask the support assistant for the status of an order.

## Lost or damaged packages

Report a lost package within 14 days of the expected delivery date. For damaged items, request a refund or a replacement within 30 days of delivery.

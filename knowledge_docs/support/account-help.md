# Account Help

## Password reset

Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. The reset link expires after 30 minutes.

## Contact

Human support agents are available Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00 Pacific time. Outside those hours the assistant can create a ticket and an agent will reply by email within one business day.

## Payment security

Never share full card numbers in chat. Support staff will never ask for your password or full card number.

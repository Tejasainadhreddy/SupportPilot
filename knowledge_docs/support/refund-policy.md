# Refund Policy

This policy applies to orders placed with Northwind Shop, a fictional store used for this demo.

## Eligibility

Delivered orders can be refunded within 30 days of the delivery date. Orders that have not been delivered yet cannot be refunded; customers can cancel them instead while they are still processing.

Digital gift cards are non-refundable.

## Amounts

The refund amount cannot be more than the amount paid for the order. Each order can be refunded only once.

## Approval

Refunds of 100 US dollars or less are processed automatically. Refunds above 100 US dollars are reviewed by a support lead, usually within one business day.

## Timing

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
